# Gameplay walkthrough

Start with Fresh arrival for the real opening economy. Later checkpoints include extra supplies for development and are not balance benchmarks.

1. Buy and place the forge blueprint, gather its construction materials and finish the forge. Its once-only grant supports the next choices.
2. Complete any two villager requests. Follow their displayed stages: open the service, perform fieldwork, then contribute. The well also needs blueprint authorization, placement and construction. Permanent projects cannot be instantly funded. Already-paid authorization costs are marked paid.
3. Discover the neighbouring village and map its walking route. Arrange the first two shipments through Journal → Trade; craft their supplies at the blacksmith. Two improvements and partnership conclude A thriving foothold.
4. Continue with A lasting trade network: finish all village improvements, reach settlement level 3, complete five deliveries and train the packhorse. Training costs 100 coins, requires 10 reputation and an idle carrier, and gives capacity 12 / speed 2.7. Completion pays 75 coins and 2 prestige once.
5. Established partners request provisions, workshop stock and water supplies in a village-specific rotation. Reputation and local influence improve these payments, capped at 20%.

## Managing work

Commission offers show wages, the selling value of consumed supplies, conditional service income, worker readiness and the pay needed to attract an uninterested eligible worker. Timing separates travel, work and recovery; estimates can change with injuries or routes. Optional support reserves another profession, changes danger weighting and can make an order wait.

Repeat last order on Guild commissions reuses its site, pay, rank and support while checking and reserving costs again. Completed site cards also offer repetition. An unavailable site or insufficient supplies blocks posting rather than bypassing the normal lifecycle.

The blacksmith accepts quantity 1–99 with eight orders maximum. Ingredients reserve immediately; cancellation refunds unfinished work. Watch active progress, change recipes while it runs and save with work queued.

Prestige funds projects. Recruitment standing preserves the highest reached prestige balance, so spending prestige never removes earned candidate rank access. Reputation improves established-partner pay and unlocks carrier training. Bookkeeping-only regional metrics are not prominent progression bars.

## Save workflow

Use named manual slots, F5 quick save and F9 quick load. Pause → Checkpoints exposes autosave settings, saved versions and backup recovery. Autosaves rotate five slots every five unpaused minutes by default. Eight prior versions of overwritten slots are kept. Loading first preserves the current ready world in Before rollback.

Check successive crop cycles, repeated cancellations, save/load mid-order and shipment recall. The next crop must retain its own full water window. Closing any window must not stop work. Notice whether required actions, waiting reasons, income and longer-term goals are understandable without consulting the code.

There is no bundled automated test suite. Inspect the game directly with development checkpoints and isolated user data.
